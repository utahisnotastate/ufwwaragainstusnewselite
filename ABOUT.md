# Why I Made This

I'm of Indian descent. My father came to the United States on an H‑1B visa. I grew up in a culture that measured kids by how hard they studied, and in American schools I got bullied and judged by report cards. I was miserable for a lot of it.

I don't want the next generation to go through that. I want kids, including Indian‑American kids growing up in strict households like mine, to meet the big ideas of physics through curiosity instead of fear. That's the idea behind what I call the **Bypass Protocol**: you shouldn't need years of grinding before you're allowed to wonder about empty space, gravity, time or light. Start with a story, ask the big question, then follow it into the real science as far as you want to go.

The lessons are written as if from a school in the year 2420, because imagining the future is the most fun way to ask these questions. Each module then shows what we actually know today, with real code you can run and check yourself.
