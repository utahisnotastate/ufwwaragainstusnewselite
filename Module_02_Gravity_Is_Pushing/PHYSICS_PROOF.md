## For the 8‑Year‑Old
"You aren’t falling down; you are being pushed in. Earth acts like an umbrella, blocking the rain of space‑pressure from below you. The rain from above pushes you down."

## Subject
Entropic gravity via mass shadowing.

## The Axiom
Gravity is not an intrinsic attraction between masses. It is a shadowing effect of an omnipresent space flux.

## The Mechanism
- A space flux (streams of virtual particles/quitons) bombards all objects from all directions.
- Two masses (M1 and M2) shield one another from this flux. Between them, pressure is reduced; outside, full pressure remains.
- The resulting pressure differential drives the masses together.

## Refutation of Drag
Standard objections (e.g., Le Sage–type drag on orbiting bodies) assume accelerated space flow. For steady, isotropic flux at constant velocity, net drag need not arise.
