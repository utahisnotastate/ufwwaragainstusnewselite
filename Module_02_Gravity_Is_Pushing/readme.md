# 📉 Module 2: Gravity Is Pushing, Not Pulling

- Lesson Title: The Shadow of the Giant
- Source: Earth‑1 Educational Archives (Year 2420)
- Concept: Entropic Gravity / Radiative Shielding

1. The Big Mistake (What Adults Think in 2025)
In 2025, teachers tell you that the Earth is like a giant magnet. They say it "pulls" you down with an invisible rope. They call this Attraction.

This is silly. How does the Earth know you are there? How does it grab you without touching you? It doesn't. There are no invisible ropes.

2. The Truth: The Umbrella Effect
Remember Module 1? We learned that Space is a Vacuum Ocean pushing on everything from all sides with huge pressure.

Imagine you are standing in a massive rainstorm. The rain is coming from everywhere—top, bottom, left, right. It is pushing you from all sides equally, so you stay in one spot.

Now, imagine a giant monster (Planet Earth) floats underneath you.

The Earth acts like a giant Umbrella.

It blocks the "rain" (pressure) coming from below you.

But there is nothing blocking the rain coming from above you.

Result:

The pressure from below is GONE (blocked by Earth).

The pressure from above is STILL THERE.

SMASH! The sky pushes you down onto the Earth.

You aren't falling. You are being pushed by the Universe against the Earth, because the Earth is hiding you from the pressure on the other side.

3. The "Two Boats" Experiment
Here is how we prove it in 2420. Imagine two toy boats floating in a bathtub. You make waves (energy) coming from all sides.

The waves hit the boats and push them around.

But look at the water between the boats. The boats block the waves from hitting each other. The water between them gets calm (flat).

The waves on the outside are still strong.

CRASH! The strong waves on the outside push the two boats together.

They didn't "attract" each other. The waves pushed them together because they were shielding each other. That is exactly what Gravity is. You and the Earth are the two boats.

4. The 2420 Homework Assignment
Question: If Gravity is just the sky pushing us down, how do we float (Anti-Gravity)? Answer: We don't need to delete the Earth. We just need to build a new umbrella. If you put a shield above your head that blocks the pressure from the sky, the push stops. You would float instantly. (This is how the "UFOs" in your picture work).
