## Subject
Chronotopology and oscillating time.

## The Axiom
Time is not a smooth, flowing river; it is a rapid oscillation (a wave). We perceive only the positive ticks (creation), missing the negative ticks (destruction) in between.

## The Mechanism
Reality is like a movie projector: a frame flashes (positive time), the shutter closes (negative/unperceived), then the next frame flashes. Continuity is an illusion created by high‑frequency flicker.

## The Implication
If the past and future are coordinates in this oscillation, then time travel is phase shifting — changing your flicker rate to match a different part of the wave.
