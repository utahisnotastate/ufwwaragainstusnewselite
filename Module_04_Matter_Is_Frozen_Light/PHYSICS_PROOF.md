## Subject
The Formon and toroidal geometrodynamics.

## The Axiom
Mass is trapped energy — frozen light. Specifically, mass is a photon rotated 90° into a complex dimension, forced to spin in a closed loop (a torus).

## The Mechanism
- The relation E = mc^2 describes photon energy (E) converting to mass (m) via a velocity resonance (c^2).
- Break the mass (fission) and you release the light; spin the light (fusion/pair production) and you create mass.

## The Formon
A Formon is a purely resonant form — a self‑sustaining wave, like a smoke ring. Matter is a collection of such resonant structures.
