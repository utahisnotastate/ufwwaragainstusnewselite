## Subject

## The Axiom
The scalar potential (φ) is not merely a passive mathematical term; it is a stress in the vacuum itself, composed of bidirectional EM wave pairs (Whittaker structure).

## The Mechanism
- Scalar interferometry uses the intersection of two non‑Hertzian (longitudinal) beams.
- Distance‑independent: These beams can traverse earth/vacuum with negligible attenuation.
- Mixing zone: Where the beams cross, they interfere. In‑phase crossings create exothermic pockets (rapid heating/low pressure). 180° out‑of‑phase crossings create endothermic pockets (rapid cooling/high pressure).

## The Proof/Implication
By arranging these high/low‑pressure zones in a grid, the jet stream can be steered electrostatically — offering an explanation for anomalous blocking highs observed historically.
